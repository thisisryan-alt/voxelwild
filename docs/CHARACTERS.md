# Characters: Human_Adam

A photoreal adult human built for real-time use: a rigged, blend-shaped, LOD'd character with
4K skin, anatomically built eyes, strand-ribbon lashes and brows, and layered hair cards. It comes with
URP shaders that match a path-traced (Cycles) reference.

Everything is generated by scripts from CC0 data. Nothing is hand-sculpted, and the whole character
rebuilds from one command. Each layer below says what it does. [Limitations](#limitations) lists what it
doesn't do yet.

## Rebuilding

```powershell
pip install bpy==5.2.1 numpy pillow scipy            # or run inside Blender 5.2.1 with --background --python
python tools/blender/human/fetch_makehuman.py         # only if SourceArt data is missing / config changed
python tools/blender/human/eye_textures.py            # iris + sclera
python tools/blender/human/build_human.py --stage full --regen --size 4096 --shots front,34,profile,eye
# Unity: Voxelwild > Art > Build Human Character   (materials + prefab)
#        Voxelwild > Art > Human Look-dev Scene    (Assets/Scenes/HumanLookdev.unity)
```

Stages: `body` (clay renders), `eyes`, `skin` (textures + look-dev, no rig), and `full` (rig, blend
shapes, LODs, FBX and textures into `Assets/`). `--size 2048` iterates about 4× faster than the final
4096. `--regen` rebuilds the skin textures. Without it, existing textures are reused.

| Output | Path |
|---|---|
| Model (all LODs, skeleton, blend shapes) | `Assets/Art/Models/Characters/Human/Human_Adam.fbx` |
| Textures | `Assets/Art/Textures/Characters/Human/` |
| Materials, prefab | `Assets/Art/Materials/Characters/Human/`, `Assets/Art/Prefabs/Characters/Human_Adam.prefab` |
| Shaders | `Assets/Shaders/Character/` |
| Generator | `tools/blender/human/` |
| Source data (CC0) | `SourceArt/Characters/Human/makehuman/` |

## What each layer does

### Anatomy (`mh.py`, `config.py`)
- MakeHuman's hm08 base mesh, morphed with the same macro rules MakeHuman uses.
- The character is a 34-year-old man, 1.79 m, lean-athletic, with mixed mostly southern-European
  ancestry.
- 28 detail targets give him an individual face: a stronger jaw and brow, a slight nasal hump, a
  fuller lower lip and hollower cheeks. Asymmetry targets make the left and right halves differ by a
  few millimetres, as real faces do.
- A new person is a new `config.py`, not new code.

### Topology and UVs (`build_human.py`)
- LOD0 is one level of Catmull-Clark on the 13k-quad base, giving 106k triangles for the body. Every
  blend shape is subdivided with it (`bl.subdivide_with_shapes`).
- Game UVs use two 0–1 tiles:
  - **head (4K):** the head island fills the tile height, with the mouth interior and nostrils beside
    it;
  - **body (4K):** torso, limbs, hands and feet.
- The face gets about 12 px/mm, enough for pores to be geometry-scale features in the normal map.

### Skin textures (`skin_textures.py`, `skin_model.py`)
Cycles bakes each texel's object-space position and normal plus the MakeHuman UV, which reads MPFB's
region masks. Everything else is evaluated in numpy at the texel's 3D position, in millimetres, in a
head-local frame. So detail is seamless across UV seams and sits in anatomically fixed places.

**Colour.** A two-layer spectral skin model (after Donner & Jensen / Jimenez):
- epidermis: eumelanin and pheomelanin;
- dermis: oxy- and deoxy-haemoglobin in Kubelka-Munk scattering;
- the result is integrated against CIE 1931 under D65.

Per-texel melanin, blood volume, oxygenation and epidermal thickness then give physically coupled
colour:
- flushed cheeks, nose and ears;
- blue-violet under-eye skin (low oxygenation, thin epidermis);
- lips;
- palms and soles (thick, low melanin);
- sun exposure on the face and forearms;
- freckles, moles and temple veins.

**Relief** (height in mm, converted to a normal map with the true mm-per-texel scale from the
position map):
- Worley pores, largest on the nose and medial cheeks;
- three scales of cross-hatched micro-relief;
- the static expression lines of a 34-year-old: forehead, glabella, crow's feet, infraorbital,
  nasolabial and neck;
- vertical lip striations;
- knuckle creases (several dorsal, one deep palmar), palm creases and fingertip ridges.

**Hair in the skin:**
- 2–3 day stubble as 3D strokes along the growth direction, plus the cool grey "five o'clock
  shadow" of shafts under the skin;
- scalp and eyebrow follicles;
- forearm, leg and chest hair.

A tiling micro-detail normal (pores and cross-hatching) covers extreme close-ups. Its tile is about
10 mm on both tiles: tiling 30 on the head and 130 on the body, because body UVs are about 5× larger per
unit.

**Masks:**
- roughness by region (oily T-zone, moist lips, wet mouth);
- cavity;
- mesh AO;
- translucency from an inner-AO thickness bake (ears and nostrils glow when back-lit).

### Eyes (`eyes.py`, `eye_textures.py`)
- The sclera sphere is fitted to MakeHuman's socket.
- The cornea and iris are true size, independent of the socket:
  - 11.8 mm iris, recessed about 0.6 mm behind the limbus and slightly conical;
  - 7.8 mm corneal curvature, with the apex where MakeHuman's own eye puts it;
  - the pupil is a real cavity.
- **Iris texture:** built in polar space, with a pupillary ruff, amber collarette zone, radial stroma,
  crypts of Fuchs, contraction furrows, limbal ring and a relief normal map.
- **Sclera texture:** off-white, with blue-grey limbal translucency, a hint of pinguecula, and vessels
  branching in from the canthi.
- **Tear meniscus strip:** found as the iso-contour where the lids meet the eyeball, stored as mesh
  edges plus interpolation factors so it follows every blend shape. It is drawn as a reflective water
  film rather than glass, since refraction through a thin strip reads as a tube.
- **Occlusion shell:** a soft contact shadow, stronger under the upper lid (vertex colour R). Path
  tracing renders it as a tinted transparent film, the same multiply the real-time shader applies.

### Hair (`strands.py`)
- **Eyelashes:** about 125 upper and 70 lower per eye, in three staggered rows. They curl, fan out,
  shorten toward the inner corner and clump.
- **Eyebrows:** about 330 hairs per brow, lying almost flat. They point up in the head of the brow,
  sideways in the body and down in the tail, with the upper and lower rows converging (herringbone).
- **Scalp:** about 1,200 cards in four layers following a combed side-part direction field. The cut
  is a short crop: about 55 mm on top, tapering to about 6 mm at the ears and nape.
  - Cards stand off the scalp in proportion to their length (volume on top, flat on the sides) and
    are randomly twisted so some catch the silhouette.
  - Cards stop where they leave the hair-covered scalp, so they never climb onto the ears.
  - Roots are sampled by scalp *area*, not by vertex, so the crown isn't left bare.
- **Hair cap:** the short taper on the sides and nape is a painted cap in the skin texture: strands
  along the same comb field and hairline as the cards (`skin_textures.scalp_cover`). This is the usual
  real-time approach, since cards that short read as flakes.
- **Card atlas:** clumped, tapered strands that thin out toward the card edges. R is root→tip, G is
  strand brightness, A is coverage.
- Lashes and brows are bound to body triangles, so they take every face blend shape.

### Rig, blend shapes and LODs (`rig_export.py`)
**Skeleton.** MPFB's CC0 `mixamo_unity` skeleton (65 bones), fitted from MakeHuman's joint cubes.
Unity's Humanoid auto-mapper recognises its Mixamo names. Eyelid and jaw bones are included. It uses
CC0 weights with 4 influences per vertex.

**Blend shapes.** 34 FACS-style expression units, named ARKit-style where they match:
- `eyeBlinkLeft`, `eyeWideLeft`, `eyeSquintLeft`;
- `browDown`, `browInnerUp`, `browOuterUp`;
- `jawOpen`, `mouthSmile`, `mouthFrown`, `mouthPucker`, `mouthFunnel`, `mouthPress`, `mouthStretch`;
- `noseSneer`, `noseFlare`;
- and more.

Teeth and tongue are re-fitted for every shape, so `jawOpen` moves the lower teeth. Tear lines,
occlusion shells, lashes and brows carry the same-named shapes.

**LODs:**

| LOD | Body | Blend shapes | Other parts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | subdivided | all | all |
| 1 | base resolution | all | all |
| 2 | 42 % decimated | none | eyes and hair only |

### Unity shaders (`Assets/Shaders/Character/`)
**`Skin`:**
- pre-integrated scattering: a curvature-wrapped, per-channel diffuse falloff;
- per-channel normal blur;
- dual-lobe GGX;
- cavity specular occlusion;
- back-lit transmission;
- vellus sheen;
- tiling micro-detail normal.

**`Eye`:**
- wrapped sclera;
- iris with a cornea caustic and pupil dilation.

**`Cornea`:** a wet, premultiplied reflection-only shell, also used for the tear meniscus.

**`EyeOcclusion`:** a multiplicative contact shadow, stronger under the upper lid.

**`Hair`:**
- Kajiya-Kay/Scheuermann dual shifted highlights;
- an alpha-tested core plus a blended soft fringe;
- back-light;
- alpha-coverage mips.

**All shaders take the voxel world's light:**
- `_VoxelSky` scales the sun, ambient and reflections;
- `_VoxelBlock` adds torch light.

**`HumanCharacter`:**
- samples both voxel light values and sets pupil dilation;
- blinks, including occasional double blinks, with the lids following a downward gaze;
- drives the eye bones toward a look target with micro-saccades.

## Verification

`tools/blender/human/validate_fbx.py` re-imports the exported FBX into an empty Blender scene. The
current export passes with no errors:

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Skeleton | 64 bones, every Humanoid-required bone plus all finger bones, jaw and eyes. Hips at 0.97 m, head at 1.64 m |
| LOD0 | 16 meshes, ~161k triangles |
| LOD1 | 16 meshes, ~81k triangles |
| LOD2 | 4 meshes, ~26k triangles |
| Blend shapes | 34 on the body. Matching shapes on lashes, brows, tear lines and occlusion shells. `jawOpen` on teeth and tongue |
| Skin weights | at most 4 influences per vertex, normalised; no unweighted vertices |
| Materials | 12 slots, named as `HumanCharacterBuilder` expects |

In Unity, `CharacterAssetTests` (EditMode) checks that:
- the five shaders compile;
- the FBX imports as a valid Humanoid avatar with an LODGroup, the expected blend shapes and two body
  submeshes;
- textures have the character import settings.

**Look-dev:** `build_human.py --shots eye,front,34,profile,macro,smile,body` renders Cycles references
under a CC0 studio HDRI plus a soft key light (`docs/images/human/`).

## Limitations

- **Generated, not scanned.** A scanned human (or MetaHuman-class data) still beats this at
  extreme close-up. The base anatomy is MakeHuman's averaged morph space, and the skin is procedural:
  convincing at portrait distance, but without a real person's idiosyncrasies. Replacing the albedo
  and normal maps with scan data is a drop-in change, using the same UVs and channels.
- **Shaders not yet verified in Unity.** This repository was built in an environment without the
  Unity editor. The Cycles renders in `docs/images/human/` are the reference; the URP shaders have not
  been compiled or captured here. Run *Build Human Character* and capture before relying on them.
- **Hair is cards, not strands.** It is tuned for a short cut. Long hair needs a different card
  layout.
- **No clothing.** The body is a smooth base mesh without genitals, ready for clothing.
- **No animation clips.** The skeleton imports as a Unity Humanoid avatar, so any Humanoid clip
  retargets onto it. MakeHuman's CC0 BVH poses target its own skeleton and are not converted.
- **4K skin textures need about 14 GB of RAM to generate.** Fields are evaluated in 2.5M-texel chunks.
  Use `--tiles Head` or `--tiles Body` to rebuild one tile.
- **No correctives.** The 34 expression units are MakeHuman's. They are not a full ARKit 52 set
  (missing, for example, `mouthLeft`/`mouthRight`, `cheekPuff` and `tongueOut`), and there are no
  corrective blend shapes for extreme joint poses.
